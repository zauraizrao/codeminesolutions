Frequently asked questions

Journal FAQs.

How to use the CodeMine journal and turn practical guidance into a clearer next step.

What topics does the CodeMine journal cover?

The journal covers website planning, web development, e-commerce, SEO, local search, branding, digital marketing, conversion improvements, and website maintenance.

Is the advice intended for small businesses?

Yes. Articles help small businesses and entrepreneurs understand practical digital decisions before, during, and after a project.

Can an article replace a technical audit or strategy?

No. Articles provide general guidance. A useful recommendation still depends on your website, market, audience, goals, budget, and available data.

Can I ask CodeMine about a topic covered here?

Yes. Link to the article when contacting us and explain how the topic relates to your business. That gives us useful context for the conversation.
