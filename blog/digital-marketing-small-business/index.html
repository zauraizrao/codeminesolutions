Frequently asked questions

Questions about this topic.

Practical answers to common questions related to this guide.

What do digital marketing services include for a small business?

Services can include strategy, website and landing page improvements, SEO, content, email, social media, paid campaigns, conversion tracking, analytics, and reporting. The right mix depends on the business goal.

Which digital marketing channel should a small business start with?

Start with the channel most closely connected to how customers discover and choose the business. Search may suit active demand, while social, email, or content may be better for awareness, trust, and repeat engagement.

How should digital marketing performance be measured?

Measure outcomes tied to the goal, such as qualified enquiries, calls, sales, bookings, revenue, conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, and repeat business, alongside supporting traffic and engagement metrics.

Does a small business need a large marketing budget?

Not necessarily. A focused plan can begin with strong foundations, one or two suitable channels, accurate tracking, and consistent improvement. Budget should reflect competition, expected value, and the time needed to gather useful data.
