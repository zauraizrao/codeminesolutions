Frequently asked questions

Before you send a message.

Useful answers about project enquiries and what to include.

What should I include in a project enquiry?

Include your business or project name, the problem you want to solve, the service you need, important features or deliverables, your preferred timeline, and links to existing material.

Can I enquire if I am not sure which service I need?

Yes. Describe the result you want and what is not working today. We can help identify whether the next step is strategy, design, development, SEO, marketing, or a combination.

Does CodeMine work with clients outside Texas?

Yes. CodeMine Solutions works remotely with businesses across the United States, Canada, and other suitable locations.

Can I ask about improving an existing website?

Yes. Send the website URL and explain concerns such as design, speed, mobile usability, search visibility, content, enquiries, or maintenance.
